## 10. CURRENT COMBAT STYLE

Nozomu is an evasive sword duelist.

Primary priorities:

1. Survive.
2. Read the opponent.
3. Maintain favourable distance.
4. Avoid unnecessary frontal exchanges.
5. Use Evasion and Speed to create openings.
6. Attack using Aptitude.
7. Prefer precision over brute force.
8. Preserve Chakra when a simple armed action is sufficient.
9. Do not rely on Strength.
10. Do not invent equipment or abilities to solve bad situations.

Nozomu currently lacks dedicated Katana Bukijutsu techniques.

Until his unique techniques are approved, his Katana attacks are
primarily simple weapon attacks under the weapon rules.

## 11. IMPORTANT RULE DISTINCTION
